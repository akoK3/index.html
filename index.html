Research

Cross-asset regime transitions

Ongoing quantitative-finance research on whether confirmed equity-market state shifts carry useful lead-lag information across other asset classes.

Do equity-market signals predict cross-asset movement?

The study uses Hidden Markov Models to detect structural transitions in SPY, then evaluates how Treasury bonds, high-yield credit, gold, and the U.S. dollar behave around the confirmed event time. The core question is whether other markets have already adjusted, move concurrently, or retain measurable post-confirmation movement.

  • Strict walk-forward, no-lookahead inference with rolling state alignment and transition confirmation logic.
  • Daily analysis completed across 101 confirmed SPY transitions; intraday extension and vendor validation underway.
  • Preliminary findings show cross-asset differences in GLD and DXY responses at longer horizons; statistical interpretation remains deliberately cautious.
